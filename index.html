A Letter For You

Something I want you to know.

My Love ♡

i love you so much, my love. more than you’ll ever know. sometimes i don’t even know how to put everything i feel into words because no matter what i say, it never feels enough. you mean so much to me, and loving you has become one of the most natural things i’ve ever done.

you’re always on my mind, whether we’re talking, laughing, or even sitting in silence. thank you for staying, for loving me, and for choosing me every single day.

i know i’m not perfect, and there are times when i make mistakes or don’t express myself the way i should, but please know that my love for you has never been fake. every “i love you” i say comes from the deepest part of my heart.

i want you to know that you’re important to me. your happiness matters, your feelings matter, and i’ll always try my best to be someone who makes you feel safe, loved, and appreciated.

i want to be the person you can come home to after a long day, the one who celebrates your happiest moments and stays by your side through the hardest ones.

if loving you means choosing you over and over again, then i’ll keep choosing you. not because i have to, but because i want to. because no matter how many people there are in this world, my heart still, finds its way back to you.

i don’t know what tomorrow holds, but one thing i’m sure of is that i’ll never stop loving you with everything i have. thank you for being my comfort, my happiness, and my favorite person.

i love you so much, my love. more than you’ll ever know, more than words could ever explain, and more than my heart could ever fully show.

always ♡
